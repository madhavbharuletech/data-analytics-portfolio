# 📊 Tableau Dashboard — Sales Performance Analysis

## 🧭 Overview

This Tableau dashboard presents an interactive view of retail sales performance across time, geography, customers, and products.  
It serves as the final reporting layer of the analytics workflow, transforming SQL-modelled data into intuitive visuals for business decision-making.

The dashboard is designed to provide a quick, executive-friendly snapshot of performance while still enabling deeper exploration.

---

## 🎯 Dashboard Objective

The dashboard enables stakeholders to:

- Monitor overall business performance  
- Identify sales growth trends  
- Understand regional contribution  
- Analyse product demand  
- Compare customer segments  

---

## 🛠️ Tool Used

- **Tableau Public**

---

## 📊 Key Metrics Displayed

- 🧾 **Total Orders** — Overall transaction volume  
- 💰 **Total Sales** — Revenue generated  
- 🧮 **Average Order Value** — Basket size indicator  
- 📅 **Average Monthly Sales** — Normalised performance trend  

These KPIs provide a high-level overview of business health.

---

## 📈 Visualisations Included

### 1️⃣ Sales Trend (Yearly)
Shows long-term revenue growth patterns and business expansion.

### 2️⃣ Monthly Sales Breakdown
Highlights seasonality and peak demand periods.

### 3️⃣ Sales by Segment
Compares revenue contribution across customer segments.

### 4️⃣ Sales by State (Map)
Provides geographic insights into regional performance.

### 5️⃣ Sales by Product
Ranks sub-categories to identify top-performing products.

---

## 🔎 Insights Enabled

- Consistent upward sales trajectory  
- Strong year-end (Q4) seasonality  
- West region as primary revenue driver  
- Technology as the leading product category  
- Balanced revenue distribution across customer segments
