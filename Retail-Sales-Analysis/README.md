## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses a multi-year retail sales dataset to uncover trends, performance drivers, and business opportunities.  
It demonstrates a complete analytics lifecycle — from exploratory analysis and SQL modelling to executive dashboard reporting.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand overall business performance  
- Analyse sales trends over time  
- Identify regional and product performance drivers  
- Evaluate customer behaviour patterns  
- Build a reusable reporting layer for dashboards

---

# 🔎 Project Methodology

This project follows a structured analytics workflow similar to real-world business analysis:

1️⃣ **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**  
Initial review of dataset structure, distributions, and key fields.

2️⃣ **Define Project Objectives**  
Clarified business questions and success metrics.

3️⃣ **Tool Mapping**  
Selected Excel for profiling, SQL for analysis, and Tableau for visualisation.

4️⃣ **Data Quality Checks**  
Validated missing values, duplicates, and consistency.

5️⃣ **Data Cleaning Decisions**  
Handled postal code gaps and ensured proper formatting.

6️⃣ **Business Question Framing**  
Defined analysis areas: trends, regions, customers, and products.

7️⃣ **SQL Analysis**  
Created KPIs, aggregations, trend queries, and reporting view  
`vw_sales_analytics`.

8️⃣ **Dashboard Planning**  
Designed KPI layout, visual hierarchy, and interactivity.

9️⃣ **Final Storytelling**  
Translated insights into actionable business narrative.

## 📈 Key Business Findings

### 🧾 Data Overview
- ~$2.26M total revenue  
- Avg. order value ≈ $459  
- Healthy transaction volume  

### ⏱️ Time Trends
- Strong growth from 2017–2018  
- Increasing order volume each year  
- Peak demand in Q4  

### 🌎 Regional Insights
- West region is the highest revenue contributor  
- South region shows consistent underperformance  

### 📦 Product Insights
- Technology is the primary revenue driver  
- Phones are the top sub-category  

### 👥 Customer Insights
- Balanced revenue distribution  
- No single dominant customer
